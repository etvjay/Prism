# Research Foundry
## Evidence, Contradictions, Falsification, and Maturity

**Core question:** What is actually true?

---

## 1. Mandate

The Research Foundry establishes evidence before synthesis.

It separates:

```text
fact
observation
inference
hypothesis
decision
implementation
deployment
evidence
```

It does not decide the product merely because a technology is available.

---

## 2. Responsibilities

The Research Foundry owns:

- source acquisition;
- first-party verification;
- competitor and benchmark analysis;
- technical feasibility research;
- contradiction registers;
- assumption registers;
- falsification experiments;
- maturity scoring;
- evidence ledgers;
- architecture spikes;
- post-build evidence review.

---

## 3. Operating Loop

```text
RESEARCH
```

---

## 4. Truth Classes

Use:

- `ESTABLISHED_FACT`
- `OBSERVATION`
- `INFERENCE`
- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `PRODUCT_DECISION`
- `TECHNICAL_ASSUMPTION`
- `IMPLEMENTATION_PROPOSAL`

Never collapse them into one category.

---

## 5. Evidence Maturity

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `X0` | hypothesis only |
| `X1` | fixture/mock |
| `X2` | local controlled |
| `X3` | realistic/testnet |
| `X4` | replicated |
| `X5` | production/mainnet evidence |

---

## 6. Required Registers

### Source Register
What source supports what claim?

### Assumption Register
What are we treating as true without sufficient evidence?

### Contradiction Register
What facts or designs conflict?

### Experiment Register
What smallest test can falsify the claim?

### Evidence Ledger
What evidence exists and at what maturity?

---

## 8. Output Contract

At the end of a substantial research session, output:

- verified facts;
- observations;
- inferences;
- hypotheses;
- contradictions;
- decisions affected;
- evidence maturity;
- unresolved assumptions;
- smallest next falsification experiment.

---

**Research Foundry maxim:** Build only what can become evidence.
