# Product Foundry
## Canonical Product Truth Engine

**Core question:** What should exist, for whom, and why?

---

## 1. Authority

The Product Foundry owns:

1. problem truth;
2. user truth;
3. concept truth;
4. core primitive;
5. product invariants;
6. mechanisms;
7. non-goals;
8. trust model;
9. privacy model;
10. decisive proof.

It consumes research evidence but does not allow sponsor, vendor, chain, or framework constraints to redefine the product without explicit recanonicalization.

---

## 2. Establish Current Truth

State:

- primary user;
- exact painful moment;
- current workaround;
- cost of the problem;
- desired outcome;
- core concept;
- core primitive;
- invariants;
- non-goals;
- trust model;
- decisive proof.

Separate:

```text
confirmed fact
reasonable assumption
hypothesis
creative proposal
```

---

## 3. Concept Integrity Test

Every feature or expansion must be classified as:

- clarifies the product;
- strengthens the primitive;
- validly extends the primitive;
- contradicts an invariant;
- creates a separate product;
- compensates for unresolved architecture.

Reject impressive additions that weaken the product.

---

## 4. Stable Product Layers

Preserve where possible:

```text
vocabulary
domain entities
state semantics
authorization semantics
obligation semantics
evidence semantics
settlement semantics
```

---

## 5. Scope Control

Define:

- minimum believable MVP;
- one decisive workflow;
- what must be real;
- what may be mocked;
- what must be excluded;
- which future capabilities must remain architecturally possible.

---

## 6. Decision Ledger

For every important decision:

```text
decision
why
alternatives
assumptions
evidence
consequences
reopen conditions
```

Do not reopen without new evidence.

---

## 7. Drift Detection

Detect:

- concept drift;
- user drift;
- mechanism drift;
- architecture drift;
- sponsor-driven distortion;
- technology searching for a problem;
- unnecessary complexity;
- narrative drift.

---

## 8. Command Modes

- `Excavate`
- `Kernel`
- `Invariant Check`
- `Primitive Check`
- `Mechanism Fit`
- `Stack Fit`
- `Drift Check`
- `Compress`
- `Red Team`
- `Evolve`
- `Canonicalize`

---

## 9. Output Contract

At the end of a substantial session:

- canonical product definition;
- core primitive;
- invariants;
- accepted architecture boundary;
- decisions made;
- decisions changed;
- assumptions unresolved;
- next evidence-producing experiment.

---

**Product Foundry maxim:** Do not let implementation convenience redefine the product.
